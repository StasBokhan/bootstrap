Our History

Formed in London in 1965, Pink Floyd evolved from a psychedelic group led by Syd Barrett into progressive rock giants under Roger Waters and David Gilmour. Their landmark 1970s concept albums—including The Dark Side of the Moon and The Wall—explored themes of isolation, mental illness, and greed. Following Waters' departure in 1985, the remaining members continued touring and recording, briefly reuniting one last time in 2005.
